How we find you

First a rough guess from your time zone, then your internet connection, and finally — if you allow it — your device's own location, which can be accurate to a few metres.

Your privacy

Nothing is stored and there is no account. To draw the page your browser asks a few public services for help: satellite imagery from Esri, place names from OpenStreetMap Nominatim and BigDataCloud, weather and elevation from Open-Meteo, and an IP lookup from ipwho.is or GeoJS.
